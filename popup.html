Answerly Inline

Tell the AI what to prioritize and how to phrase its answer. This is saved on this device and sent with your question when you use a shortcut. The AI returns structured data internally; the page shows the answer, a brief explanation, and any supported citations.

Study module files

Drop PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, or Markdown files here

Up to 10 files, 20 MB each, and 300,000 extracted characters per file. Scanned image-only PDFs may not contain extractable text.

    Alt + Q Select a matching radio choice
    Alt + W Insert a written answer below the question
    Alt + R Then click an image question to answer it
    Alt + 1 Remove Answerly text and markers

    For Alt+R, press the shortcut, then click the visible image/question. Answerly captures only a crop around your click and sends it to Deep Relay with nearby page text. Choose Kimi K3 or GLM 5.3 Flash for image answers. Press Escape before clicking to cancel.

    Files are read locally; only extracted text is stored. Relevant passages are sent to Deep Relay only when you request an answer. Without files, answers use model knowledge; Deep Relay does not provide web search, so answers are not live-researched.

    Highlight the question before using Alt+Q or Alt+W. For image questions, press Alt+R first and click the image. Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
